I'm a theoretical computer scientist and multidisciplinary engineer driven by curiosity about complex systems, and the challenge of turning ambitious ideas into working technology.

My work spans real-time graphics, simulation, compilers, embedded systems, robotics, and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on high-performance computing and systems engineering. At the centre of much of this exploration is **XII**, a C++23 real-time engine I've been developing to push my understanding of software architecture and computational systems.

Beyond engineering, I lead technical communities, mentor aspiring engineers, and create opportunities for others to learn and innovate. My Christian faith grounds my principles, shaping my approach to leadership, service, and responsibility.

I believe that even the most difficult problems are worth pursuing when approached with curiosity, discipline, and determination.

*With sufficient discipline and tenacity, we can make impossibilities possible.*
