# About

I'm Theophilus Eriata, a software engineer who builds systems where correctness and performance matter. My work sits at the intersection of graphics programming, real-time simulation, robotics, and AI — but the thread running through everything is simple: I'm drawn to problems where precision is not optional.

I'm currently completing a Computer Science (Honours) degree at the University of Calgary, with focus areas in theoretical CS, graphics, and systems. Outside of coursework, I work on XII Engine — a cross-platform C++23 real-time engine — and on research connecting GPU-accelerated simulation to control problems in robotics and biomedical engineering.

I've always been more curious about how people grow than how systems scale. That's why mentoring students through FIRST Robotics, teaching at church, and building technical communities at the University of Calgary have become meaningful parts of my life alongside the engineering work itself. I enjoy environments where people discover what they're capable of — and I try to create those environments wherever I can.

Service has shaped me as much as engineering. Volunteering with Alberta Health Services and the Mustard Seed taught me how powerful presence can be: listening carefully, guiding without overreaching, helping someone navigate a difficult moment. Those experiences grounded me and made me want to work in fields where technical skill and human care are genuinely inseparable.

Faith gives me direction. Engineering gives me tools. Community gives me purpose. Together, they've shaped how I approach every project and every person — with patience, curiosity, and a commitment to building things that genuinely help.

*With sufficient discipline and tenacity, we can make impossibilities possible.*
